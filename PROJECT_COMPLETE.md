## 📋 What Was Built

A complete, premium, production-ready website for **Sri Ganapathy Traders** - a wholesale and retail construction materials supplier in Nagercoil, Tamil Nadu.

### All 6 Pages Completed:

1. **Home** (`/`) - Cinematic hero section with full-screen background, animated product highlights, brand showcase, features section, and strong CTAs
2. **Products** (`/products`) - Complete product catalog with high-quality images, descriptions, and WhatsApp enquiry buttons for each product
3. **Brands** (`/brands`) - Detailed showcase of all trusted brands with features, benefits, and product information
4. **About Us** (`/about`) - Company introduction, values, services, statistics, and mission
5. **Wholesale** (`/wholesale`) - Dedicated page for bulk enquiries with category-specific quote requests
6. **Contact** (`/contact`) - Comprehensive contact page with functional form, WhatsApp integration, phone links, and Google Maps
